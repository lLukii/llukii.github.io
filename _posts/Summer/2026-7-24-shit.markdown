March 2026. In the span of just three days, I witnessed the culmination of my junior year extracurriculars come tumbling down to the ground before my eyes. After pouring my heart and soul into robotics, my hopes of a glorious season finale was swiftly shot down by probably the worst championship throw of all time. A day later, I found out that I didn't even make it to the NYC finals of Terra after pouring more than three whole months trying to build a CBIO project. The cherry on top, I was one spot away from FIRST Leadership Finalist. 6 months of work, and what remained on the outside was nothing but a spec of dust.  

Awards and recognition aren't everything, but I have the right to be frustrated for not attaining a goal I set. Maybe I did deserve this: I spent so much time on robotics that I pretty much threw my entire research presentation; when championships finally rolled around, my sheer carelessness was largely to blame for our abysmal stats at championships on paper. Maybe it was never an issue with my laziness or intellect—I showed up to every team meeting and put in the work on my paper. Maybe it was because I seized the wrong moments. In other words, I started the marathon as the fastest runner, but lost steam the earliest. 

Maybe none of this would've happened had I conserved my energy for the final mile. Maybe I would've made it to finals—or even ISEF—if I had taken the time to carefully plan my research poster in the weeks leading up to preliminary round. Maybe I would've ended up with something better for the summer had I crafted a well-concieved cold email template. All these things were within my reach, but I failed to obtain them due to poor execution...

But no, I won't let this be the end of me. I won't, no matter what. I don't care if I have fifteen essays to write, or that I'm living alone in a hotel in California. Reddit might say that I'm late to the party, but there's still an ounce of time this summer. The best time might've been three months ago, but the second best time is now.

Recently, I listened to a Kasane Teto song that ended with this verse
> I feel like shit, but I'm okay [ohhh]

Maybe I'm still stupid for aiming so high, but I guess the red-haired chimera has a point: as much as a lot of shit has happened to me so far, I think the dots will connect at some point...
